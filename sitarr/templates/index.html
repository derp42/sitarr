How age guidance becomes a household rating

These are Sitarr’s US household bands. They do not claim to re-certify a film. Exact-age tags add finer control for protected viewers.

Movies

0–5 → G
6–10 → PG
11–13 → PG-13
14–17 → R
18–21 → NC-17

TV shows

0–5 → TV-Y
6–7 → TV-Y7
8–10 → TV-PG
11–14 → TV-14
15–21 → TV-MA
